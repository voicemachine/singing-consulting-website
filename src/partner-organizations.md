Blake regularly collaborates with early-music ensembles and partner organizations across the
country. One frequent collaborator is **Tonos del Sur**, a Bloomington-based ensemble
specializing in Baroque-era music from the Americas, led by violinist Sarah Elizabeth Cranor.

[Learn more about Tonos del Sur →](https://www.sarahcranor.com/tonos)
